Why Æmber?

A coal that went underground and kept glowing.

Æmber is the ember of the Aether: a coal that went underground and kept glowing. Like the Feminine. Like Indigenous Knowing. Like all of Life itself.

I'm Julie. The work I do, in every form, is bringing people back into their identities, so their Self and their Shine can come back online instead of living in fear, anxiety and judgment.
