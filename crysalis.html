How I see you

I hold up the mirror.

I'm a Pisces with a Scorpio moon. I see you, clearly and deeply, and I reflect back the unique beauty I see, until you see it too and believe it.

With an ocean of empathy, I listen for your vision, your voice, and your dreams, including the ones you haven't let yourself admit yet. I hear what you want, and I give you permission to want it.

And I'm a catalyst. I hold unwavering belief in you, in your dreams, and in your worthiness of both. Then I say: Go ahead. Be it. Do it. I believe in you.

The bigger picture

This work is never only personal.

Individually and collectively, we're in the middle of a huge transition. We're finding ourselves, our beautiful selves, and we're trying to see a better way.

When one person comes home to their own values, and lives by them along with the values of Life itself, the whole web shifts. A leader who knows what they stand for doesn't get pulled into fear and scarcity when power arrives. They protect what matters: health, joy, abundance, and flourishing for all living things.

Every person who steps fully into themselves is a quiet act of revolution.

"To have wings, and yet not fly, would be a tragedy of the tallest order."
