Language & Identity

Your voice,
in any language.

English is the vehicle. Your voice is the point.

More than English lessons

I give you yourself back.

I teach English, and what I'm really teaching is the courage to be yourself out loud. I have a master's in TESOL and years of experience with adults from all over the world, and my students usually tell me our lessons feel like something more.

Living in a culture that isn't your own can make you feel small, or like a different person than you really are. In our work together, we build your English, and we also make room for all of you: your humor, your warmth, your intelligence, and your own way of being in the world.

What I believe
