Why I teach

The best gift you can give someone

I started dancing three years ago, looking for my passion, and found myself. I believe dance is one of the greatest gifts a person can give, second only to permission and confidence to be oneself.

When I teach, I'm not just teaching steps. I'm watching for the moment you stop thinking and start dancing, when your own style shows up. That's my favorite moment, every time.

Ways to dance with me

Offerings

Couples

Your Wedding Dance

Your first dance should feel like the two of you. Together we'll create something that fits your song, your style, and your comfort level, so on the day you can relax, enjoy each other, and shine.

Everyone

Ballroom Lessons

Gentle, joyful introductions for brand-new beginners, and room to grow for dancers who want more. We go at your pace, build your confidence on the floor, and find your own expression inside the steps.

For dance instructors

Teaching the Teachers

Most dance teachers can dance beautifully. Far fewer have studied how people actually learn. With a master's degree in teaching and years in the classroom, I train instructors to teach so students feel safe, seen, and free:

  • How adults learn new movement
  • Reaching students who learn in different ways
  • Building confidence in nervous beginners
  • Giving clear, kind feedback that sticks
  • Creating a class where everyone feels welcome
