Say hello

Hi, I'm Julie

A dancer, a teacher, and a seer of people.

I'm a ballroom dancer, an English teacher with a master's in TESOL, a student of culture, a poet, and a guide for people in transition. That might sound like a lot of different things. To me, it's all one thing.

The work I do, in every form, is bringing people back into their identities, so their Self and their Shine can come back online instead of living in fear, anxiety, and judgment. I look at you the way you'd look at a seed, and I already see the tree.

My students often tell me, "You're like my therapist." I think what they mean is that with me, they get to be themselves. And that's where the magic starts.

Æmber is the ember of the Aether: a coal that went underground and kept glowing. Like the Feminine. Like Indigenous Knowing. Like all of Life itself.

What I believe

Everything in nature is exactly what it is. The flower doesn't apologize for blooming. When we shine as ourselves, and stay connected to each other and to all of Life, that is the most powerful thing we can do.
