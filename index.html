Why AEmber?

AEmber is the ember of the Aether: a coal that went underground and kept glowing. Like the Feminine. Like Indigenous Knowing. Like all of Life itself.

I'm Julie. The work I do, in every form, is bringing people back into their identities, so their Self and their Shine can come back online instead of living in fear, anxiety and judgment.

Confidence in Communication

English is the vehicle. Your voice is the point.

I teach English, and what I'm really teaching is the courage to be yourself out loud. Learners come for grammar and fluency, and leave speaking as who they are.

  • Speak as yourself

    Conversation that builds fluency and ease, without fear of getting it wrong.

  • Clear & expressive

    Grammar and writing that serve your meaning, not the other way around.

  • Goals that matter

    Exam, career and life preparation, with your confidence at the center.

Dance

The greatest gift, besides permission to be yourself.

I dance, and I love helping people come into dance for the first time. I believe it's one of the greatest gifts a person can give, second only to permission and confidence to be oneself.

  • Dance for everyone

    Gentle, joyful introductions to movement, whatever your experience.

  • For dance instructors

    I'm developing training for teachers: how to teach so students feel safe, seen and free.
